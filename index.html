About Brett

Built in the Classroom. Scaled in the Boardroom.

I started as a public school teacher in California. That's where I learned the thing most learning programs forget: people don't change because content exists. They change because something makes the effort worth it.

I carried that into 25 years of building learning organizations inside high-growth SaaS, including VP roles at Coupa, Equinix, and Comcast. I launched global academies, built a Sales Academy, turned education into a revenue line, and owned the P&L that proved it.

Today I help leaders put AI to work in learning without losing what makes learning stick. And I build the tools myself, because the fastest way to separate AI hype from AI value is to ship something and watch what happens.

  • M.A. Educational Technology, San Diego State University
  • B.A. Social Psychology, UCLA
  • California Teaching Credential, National University
  • Half marathoner, practicing Stoic, and convinced that good teaching starts with empathy, even when there's an AI in the room.
